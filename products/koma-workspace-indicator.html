kOMA Workspace Indicator

Keep your desktops close at hand. Switch workspaces, see which applications are open, and choose a compact panel view that fits your desktop.

Part of kOMA, inspired by Omarchy and made for KDE Plasma. Built on Desktop Switcher by Sm1Tee, with kOMA appearance and behavior changes.

Your workspaces, at a glance

Switch from the panel

Click a workspace to switch to it, or scroll through your virtual desktops without leaving the panel.

See your applications

Show application icons with workspace numbers. Indicators refresh when windows move between desktops.

Choose your look

Use plain separators for a minimal view, or choose shapes, colors, and sizes in the appearance settings.

Fit your panel

Compact sizing works with horizontal and vertical panels. Use Plasma theme colors or choose your own.

Get kOMA Workspace Indicator

Download version 0.2.1, then use Plasma’s widget manager to Install Widget From Local File. Select the downloaded .plasmoid and add kOMA Workspace Indicator to your panel.

Download the widget → Source & support →

Requires KDE Plasma 6. Existing kOMA installations retain the kde-desktop.workspaces plugin ID.

Built on Desktop Switcher

Original development by Sm1Tee. kOMA modifications by Greg / Columbia Foundry add plain separators, numbers alongside icons, compact sizing, English defaults, and updates when windows move. We could not locate an upstream source repository; the original widget’s metadata declares GPL-2.0-or-later, which we preserve.
