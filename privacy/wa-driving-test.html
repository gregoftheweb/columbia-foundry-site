WA Driving Test (Unofficial): Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

WA Driving Test (Unofficial) is a practice quiz for the Washington State driver knowledge test, made by Columbia Foundry LLC ("we", "our", or "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. Our general privacy policy covers our website and other apps.

The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Washington State Department of Licensing or any official driver knowledge test. It does not issue, process or affect any license, test or government record.

The app keeps your data on your phone

There are no accounts and no sign-in, and the app never asks for your name, email or any other personal details. Your settings are stored only on your device, and your test answers are kept only while you take a test. We do not collect them, and they are deleted when you delete the app. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Advertising

The app is free and shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen. The ads are served by Google AdMob and are limited to content suitable for teens. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • the device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking);
  • the IP address, and the approximate location that can be worked out from it;
  • device and app information, such as the device type, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with the ads, such as views and taps.

Google uses this information under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

Your choices

  • On iPhone, the app asks whether it may track you. If you say no, or turn tracking off later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • In the EU, the UK and other places where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the app's About page ("Privacy and ad choices").
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Links to other websites

The app links to the official Washington Driver Guide on the Washington State Department of Licensing website (dol.wa.gov) and to a Washington state law on the Washington State Legislature website (app.leg.wa.gov). These open in your browser and are governed by those websites' own privacy policies.

Children and teens

The app is meant for people preparing for a driver knowledge test, including teens getting a learner permit. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. The app itself collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
