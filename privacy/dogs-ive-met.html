Dogs I’ve Met: Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

Dogs I’ve Met is an app for remembering the dogs you meet, made by Columbia Foundry LLC ("we", "our", or "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. Our general privacy policy covers our website and other apps.

Your dogs stay on your phone

There are no accounts and no sign-in. Everything you enter, such as dog names, breeds, owners' names, where you met, notes, dates and photos, is stored only on your device. We do not collect it or upload it anywhere, and it is deleted when you delete the app. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Camera and photos

If you choose to add a picture of a dog, the app asks for permission to use your camera or your photo library. The picture you take or pick is kept in the app on your device. The app does not look at, copy or upload any other photos.

Voice dictation

You can fill in a field by speaking instead of typing. The app asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition, and only listens while you are dictating. The speech is turned into text by your phone's own speech recognition service (Apple's on iPhone, Google's on Android), which may process the audio on the device or send it to Apple or Google to transcribe, under their privacy policies. We do not receive or keep the audio.

Advertising

The app is free and shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen. The ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • the device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking);
  • the IP address, and the approximate location that can be worked out from it;
  • device and app information, such as the device type, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with the ads, such as views and taps.

Google does not receive your dogs, notes or photos. Google uses this information under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

Your choices

  • On iPhone, the app asks whether it may track you. If you say no, or turn tracking off later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • In the EU, the UK and other places where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings page ("Privacy and ad choices").
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.
  • You can turn camera, photo, microphone and speech recognition access on or off at any time in your device's settings.

Children

Dogs I’ve Met is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
