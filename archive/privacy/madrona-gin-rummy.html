Madrona Gin Rummy: Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

Madrona Gin Rummy is a card game made by Columbia Foundry LLC ("we", "our", or "us") in partnership with Madrona Distillery. This policy explains what the app does with information. Our general privacy policy covers our website and other apps.

The game keeps your data on your phone

There are no accounts and no sign-in. Your saved game, settings, statistics and your answer to the age question are stored only on your device. We do not collect them, and they are deleted when you delete the app. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Advertising

The app is free and shows a banner ad at the bottom of the game table. The ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • the device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking);
  • the IP address, and the approximate location that can be worked out from it;
  • device and app information, such as the device type, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with the ads, such as views and taps.

Google uses this information under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

Your choices

  • On iPhone, the app asks whether it may track you. If you say no, or turn tracking off later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • In the EU, the UK and other places where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the app's About page ("Privacy and ad choices").
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Age

Madrona Gin Rummy carries a spirits brand and is intended for adults 21 and over. The app asks your age before play. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. The game involves no real money, betting or prizes.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
